# 10. Department Structure

Initial departments:

```text
Quixotic AI
│
├── Company Wide
├── Administration
├── HR
├── Sales
├── Marketing
├── AI
├── Development
├── Finance
├── Customer Support
└── Operations
```

Admin can:

- Create departments
- Rename departments
- Archive departments
- Assign users
- Assign knowledge to one or more departments
- Set department managers
